# World Boss

Check the world boss spawn timetable, and get notified when a spawn is coming up or happening.

## What you can do

- See the current, previous, and next world boss spawn info
- Get notified some minutes before a spawn, and the moment it happens
- Exclude bosses you're not interested in
- <Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" /> Show the next boss info on top of your game screen via [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/)

## How to use it

1. Open the World Boss screen to see the closest upcoming boss (current), the one after that (next), and the one that just passed (previous). Bosses that spawn at the same time are grouped together.
2. Turn on notifications in the settings screen (off by default). Once enabled, you can add how many minutes in advance you want to be notified (defaults: 1, 5, and 10 minutes before). You can add up to **10** reminder times.
3. You'll also get a single notification the moment a boss spawns.
4. You can exclude bosses you don't care about from the settings screen. Excluded bosses won't trigger notifications and are left out of the current/next boss calculation.

## Good to know

- Spawn times are shown based on the region selected in [Settings](/features/settings/); switching regions changes the boss list too.
- Servers with daylight saving time (North America/Europe) have their spawn times adjusted automatically.
- Limited-time event bosses appear in the list along with their start/end period when active.
- If the notification switch is off, you won't get near-spawn or spawn notifications at all — check this first if notifications seem to not be working.
- If you have the [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/) turned on (Windows), the next boss info calculated here is also shown live on your game screen.
