# Broadcast Widgets

Transparent-background widgets meant to be added as a "Browser Source" in OBS or similar broadcasting software.

## What you can do

- Show time remaining until Patrigio (Hidden Merchant) appears, or whether it's currently available
- Display a widget that overlays naturally on top of your broadcast

## How to use it

1. Add a "Browser" source in OBS or similar software.
2. Enter the URL of a Karanda broadcast widget page (e.g., the Patrigio widget) as the source.
3. The background renders transparent, so only the widget appears over your broadcast.

### Patrigio widget

- Patrigio appears every 4 hours in-game time — at **00:40, 04:40, 08:40, 12:40, 16:40, and 20:40**.
- During in-game night (22:00–10:00), a green border and a "Hidden shop available!!" message is shown.
- During the day, time remaining until the next spawn is shown (seconds if under a minute, minutes otherwise).

## Good to know

- The widget needs a transparent background to composite naturally in your broadcast — don't set a background color in your OBS browser source settings.
- No login is required to use it.
- A simple clock widget is also available, and more broadcast widgets are planned for the future.
