# Party Finder

Post, browse, apply to, and manage party/platoon/guild recruitment posts.

## What you can do

- Post and browse recruitment for Party/Platoon, Guild Boss Raid, and Guild War categories
- Apply to a post, or cancel your application
- As the author, accept or reject applicants
- See a real-time updating list of recruitment posts
- <Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" /> Check active recruitment posts on top of your game screen via [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/)

## How to use it

### Finding and joining a post

1. Open Party Finder to see currently active posts, with open ones first and the most recently created ones at the top.
2. Tap a post you're interested in to see its details. If you're logged in, you'll also see private details meant only for applicants (like a Discord link).
3. Tap Apply to send your application to the author. You can also cancel your application afterward.

### Creating and managing a post

1. When creating a post, fill in region, recruitment category (Party/Platoon, Guild Boss Raid, Guild War), recruitment type, max members, spec requirements, and so on. You can split content into public info and applicant-only private info (like a Discord link).
2. Review your applicants and accept or reject each one.
3. Close the post once recruitment is done, or reopen it later if needed.

### Notification settings

- In the in-screen notification settings, turn on Party Finder alerts (off by default), and exclude categories you're not interested in.

## Good to know

- Private details (like Discord links) aren't visible if you're not logged in. You need to be [logged in](/features/auth/) to apply.
- On the web, real-time updates pause while you're away from the screen and resume when you come back. On Windows, the connection stays live regardless.
- On Windows, active recruitment posts are also shown live in the [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/) — you can exclude categories you don't want to see there.
- Posts hidden due to reports appear dimmed in the list.
