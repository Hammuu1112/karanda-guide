# Login

Log in to Karanda with your Discord account, and register your Black Desert Family so you can verify you own it.

## What you can do

- Log in easily with your Discord account (no separate sign-up needed)
- Register/unregister your Black Desert Family
- Verify in-game that you actually own the Family
- Log out / delete your account

## How to use it

### Logging in

1. Tap the login button in the top-right corner of the home screen.
2. You'll be taken to the Discord authorization screen. Karanda only asks to identify your Discord account (nickname/avatar) — approve it and you're logged in.
3. Once logged in, you'll stay logged in on future launches until you explicitly log out. The web version automatically retries login every time you open the page.

### Registering a Family

1. After logging in, choose "Register Family" on the login info screen.
2. Enter your region (KR/NA/EU/JP) and Family name to register it to your account.
3. If you registered the wrong Family or want to change it, unregister the existing one first, then register the new one. Only one Family can be registered per account.
4. To refresh your Family data, use the refresh option.

### Family ownership verification

Registering a Family doesn't automatically prove you own it. To verify ownership:

1. Start "Ownership Verification" on the login info screen.
2. Lock the item indicated on screen in-game (either a life skill or your contribution points).
3. Once you've done that in-game, come back and complete the verification.
4. You have **10 minutes** from when you start verification to finish it. If time runs out, verification expires and you'll need to start over.

### Logging out / deleting your account

- You can log out or delete your account from the login info screen. Deleting your account logs you out immediately and takes you to the home screen. This can't be undone, so proceed carefully.

## Good to know

- If a login status check fails, you may be logged out automatically — the app can't tell whether it's a server issue or an expired token. If you're suddenly logged out, just log in again.
- On Windows, logging in opens your default browser, and once authorization finishes it automatically returns you to the app (or a Discord channel). This briefly uses port 8082 on your PC, so login can fail if another program is already using that port.
- The 10-minute limit for ownership verification is just a display hint — the server determines actual expiry based on when verification started.
