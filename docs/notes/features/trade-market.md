# Trade Market

Check real-time Black Desert trade market prices and the registration waiting list, and quickly look up frequently used item presets.

## What you can do

- Search for an item by name to see its detailed price (including price history by enhancement level)
- See the real-time list of items currently waiting to be registered on the market
- View prices at a glance for popular presets like Melody of Stars, Magical Lightstone Crystal, Dehkia's Light, and Essence of Dawn
- Calculate cooking box materials based on your contribution points and proficiency

## How to use it

### Search and price detail

1. Type an item's name in the trade market search box to get live results (whitespace is ignored, partial matches work).
2. Select an item to view its detail page. You can check the highest/lowest/median price and price history per enhancement level, and switch between enhancement levels.
3. Use the region selector (KR/NA/EU/JP) on the detail page to check prices on a different server.

### Waiting list

- The main trade market screen shows a real-time list of items waiting to be registered. Items that aren't tradable on the market at all won't appear here.
- If you change your region in [Settings](/features/settings/), the waiting list reconnects to that region.

### Presets

1. Pick a preset (Melody of Stars, Magical Lightstone Crystal, Dehkia's Light, Essence of Dawn) from the top menu.
2. All items in that preset are looked up at once. Items in stock are shown first, and among those, cheaper unit prices come first.

### Cooking box calculator

1. Go to the cooking box preset screen. You can pick one of 6 box types (default: the ingredient-cost box).
2. Enter your contribution points (default 200) and proficiency (default 1200).
   - **Number of deliveries = contribution points ÷ 2**, calculated automatically.
   - Your proficiency determines your silver bonus tier.
3. The material list is also sorted by stock availability and total cost (unit price × quantity needed).

## Good to know

- The default region follows your [Settings](/features/settings/) region. To check another server, change the region directly on the detail/preset screen.
- On the web version, the waiting list disconnects in real time when you leave the screen, and reconnects when you come back (this saves data). On Windows, the connection stays alive even when you're not on the screen.
- "Latest price" on the detail screen always refers to the most recent date's price.
- The rule that 2 contribution points allow 1 cooking box delivery is a game rule and is reflected exactly as-is.
