# Shutdown Scheduler

<Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" />

Schedule your PC to shut down automatically at a time you choose.

## What you can do

- Schedule an automatic shutdown at a specific time (hour:minute)
- Cancel a scheduled shutdown
- See a live countdown to shutdown when one is scheduled

## How to use it

1. Pick the time (hour:minute) you want on the Shutdown Scheduler screen.
2. Tap "Start" to schedule the shutdown — a live countdown to shutdown appears.
3. Tap the red "Cancel" button to cancel; the button switches back to "Start."

## Good to know

- If the time you picked has already passed, or is less than a minute away, it's automatically scheduled for the same time tomorrow instead. For example, picking 11:55 PM when it's already 11:59 PM schedules it for 11:55 PM the next day.
- Shutdown happens as a forced shutdown — save any work (like open documents) before it triggers.
- If you close and reopen the app, the screen still shows your previous scheduled state, but the actual OS-level shutdown schedule is managed separately by Windows. If you're not sure it's still accurate, cancel and reschedule to be safe.
- On PCs with restricted administrator permissions, the shutdown command may not work as expected.
