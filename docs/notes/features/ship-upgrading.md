# Ship Upgrading

Calculate the materials needed to upgrade ships like the Caravel or Epheria Sailboat, and track your progress against your current stock.

## What you can do

- See the total materials needed for every part of the ship you selected
- See your remaining materials needed and completion rate, based only on parts you haven't finished yet
- Enter and manage how many of each material you have (up to 9999)
- Add materials earned from the daily quest in one tap

## How to use it

1. Select the ship you want to upgrade at the top of the screen.
2. You'll see the parts needed for that ship, and the materials needed for each part. Enter how many of each material you currently have, or adjust with the +/- buttons.
3. Tap the "Daily Quest" button to add the fixed amount of each of the 17 materials you earn from the daily quest, all at once. If you've completed the daily quest, this button is all you need.
4. Once all materials for a part are filled, that part is marked as complete. Materials used for completed parts are excluded from the completion rate at the top, so you can focus on the parts you still need.

## Good to know

- The stock quantities you enter are saved automatically, so they persist even after you close and reopen the app.
- Each material's stock can go up to 9999 — it won't increase past that.
- Materials for parts already marked complete are excluded from the completion rate, so it's normal for the overall rate not to reach 100% even when you've finished several parts (their share has already been removed).
- If a game update changes ships, parts, or required materials, the calculations here are updated to match.
