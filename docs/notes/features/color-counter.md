# Color Counter

A simple tool for counting red, yellow, blue, and white — handy for counting colors while playing.

## What you can do

- Quickly tap to count colors in-game

## How to use it

1. Tap a color box to increase its count by 1 (no upper limit).
2. Tap the button below a box to decrease it by 1 (won't go below 0).
3. Tap the refresh button in the bottom corner to reset all four colors to 0.

## Good to know

- Counts aren't saved — leaving the screen resets everything back to 0. It's meant as a lightweight tool for quick counting during gameplay.
- The layout adjusts automatically to your screen's aspect ratio: boxes arrange horizontally on a wide screen and vertically on a tall one.
