# Home

This is the dashboard you see first when you launch Karanda. From here you can log in, jump to any feature, and check the latest news.

## What Karanda offers

The icons in the middle of the home screen take you straight to these features:

- [Ship Upgrading](/features/ship-upgrading/) — Calculate the materials needed to upgrade your ship and track your stock.
- [News](/features/news/) — Check official BDO notices, updates, and events, and get real-time notifications.
- [Lightstone Combination](/features/lightstone-combination/) — Search lightstone combinations for the effect you want.
- [Bartering](/features/bartering/) — Calculate parley consumption and trade good weight for bartering.
- [Shutdown Scheduler](/features/shutdown-scheduler/) <Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" /> — Schedule your PC to shut down automatically at a set time.
- [Color Counter](/features/color-counter/) — A simple tool for counting colors while playing.
- [Trade Market](/features/trade-market/) — Search item prices and check the real-time waiting list and popular presets.
- [World Boss](/features/world-boss/) — Check the world boss spawn timetable and get near-spawn alerts.
- [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/) <Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" /> — Overlay the clock, world boss info, party recruitment, and more on top of your game screen.

[Login](/features/auth/), [Party Finder](/features/party-finder/), and [Broadcast Widgets](/features/broadcast-widgets/) don't have a home screen icon — you use them by visiting their pages directly.

## How to use the home screen

- Check your Discord login status in the top-right corner and log in with the button there.
- Tap a feature icon in the middle to jump straight to it. The number of icons per row adjusts automatically to your window width.
- The news section shows a carousel of events whose deadline is within 3 days first; if there aren't any, it shows the latest 5 events instead. Cards for the latest major update and lab news appear alongside it.
- At the bottom you'll find links to frequently used external sites such as the official homepage, Inven, Inven Map, Garmoth, Bdocodex, and bdolytics.
- The gear icon in the top-right takes you to [Settings](/features/settings/); "Support Karanda" at the bottom takes you to the support page; "Contact" opens a Google Form.

## Good to know

- <Badge type="warning" text="Windows only" /> The [In-game Overlay](/features/overlay/) and [Shutdown Scheduler](/features/shutdown-scheduler/) icons only work on the Windows app. On web/Android, tapping them just shows a "Windows only" message instead of navigating.
- On Windows, if you turn on "Minimize to tray" in [Settings](/features/settings/), closing the window won't quit the app — it stays in the tray (the small icon near your taskbar clock). Left-click the tray icon to bring the window back, or right-click for a show/hide/quit menu.
- Window size and position are saved automatically while you use the home screen, and restored the next time you launch the app — no need to set this yourself.
- A notice at the very bottom always reminds you that Karanda is an unofficial fan service, not an official Pearl Abyss product.
