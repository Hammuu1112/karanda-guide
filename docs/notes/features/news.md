# News

Check official Black Desert notices, updates, and events, along with lab news, all in one place — and get real-time notifications for new ones.

## What you can do

- Check the latest major update and lab news
- Browse ongoing events (with time remaining until deadline)
- Sort events by registration date or deadline, and choose whether to include ongoing (no-deadline) events
- Get real-time notifications for new news

## How to use it

1. At the top of the News screen, check the latest major update and lab news cards. If there's nothing to show, the card is simply hidden.
2. Below that, the event list shows a "D-N" badge for time remaining until each deadline. It turns red when the deadline is within 3 days, blue otherwise. Events with no deadline show "Ongoing" instead.
3. Use the filter icon on the right to change how the list is sorted.
   - **By registration date** (default): most recently registered events first.
   - **By deadline**: ongoing events first (soonest deadline first), then ended events (most recently ended first).
   - **Include ongoing** checkbox (on by default): turn it off to hide events with no deadline.
4. Tap any item to open the official Black Desert page for it.
5. Pull down to refresh and fetch the latest news.
6. To get notified, turn on news notifications in [Settings](/features/settings/) and choose the categories you want (Notice/Update/Event/Lab).

## Good to know

- The home screen also highlights events with a deadline coming up in 1–3 days, labeled "Events near deadline." If there aren't any, it shows the latest 5 events labeled "Events" instead.
- The deadline day itself (D-0) counts as "ended," not "ongoing." Ended events aren't removed from the list — they just appear dimmed.
- News notifications are off by default. You need to turn them on yourself in [Settings](/features/settings/).
- Lab news is shown regardless of region.
- Event deadlines are calculated based on when our crawler first detected the post, which may differ from the event's actual start date.
